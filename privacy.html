Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026

  • Health Sync runs on your own computer. There is no Health Sync account and no Health Sync server.
  • Your Google Health data goes directly from Google to your computer and stays on your own devices. The developer never receives it.
  • Your data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone.

Data Health Sync reads from Google

With your permission, Health Sync gets read-only access to your Google Health data. It cannot change or delete anything in your Google account. Depending on what your devices record, it reads:

  • Activity and fitness: steps, distance, calories, active minutes, active zone minutes, heart rate zones, exercise sessions and cardio fitness.
  • Health metrics and measurements: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate, skin temperature and, if a glucose sensor is linked to Google Health, blood glucose.
  • Sleep: sleep sessions and sleep stages.
  • Profile: read once, right after you sign in, to confirm that your account has Google Health data. It is shown to you and not saved.

It also saves the name of the device or app that recorded each value (for example, your Fitbit Air).

How Health Sync uses it

Only to show you your own data:

  • to save it as files on your computer that you can open in a spreadsheet;
  • to build a private view of your day on your computer (for example, when you slept, worked, ate or travelled);
  • to learn your personal labels for that view. This learning happens on your computer, and what it learns stays there.

Health Sync does not use your data for advertising, does not sell it, does not build profiles for anyone else, and does not use it to train any model other than your personal one on your own computer.

Where it is stored

On your computer, in the Health Sync folder, as ordinary files. Your Google sign-in token is stored there too. The files are not encrypted by Health Sync and are kept until you delete them, so protect your computer with a password and, ideally, disk encryption (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS).

Who it is shared with

No one. The developer has no access to your data, and Health Sync does not send your Google Health data to any other company or service.

If you choose to open your Health Sync pages on your phone or another of your own devices, for example through a private network such as Tailscale, your data travels over that encrypted connection between your devices. That connection is something you set up and control.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Health Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data you can choose to add

These sources are optional and are processed on your computer:

  • Computer activity: which apps and windows were in use, recorded locally by ActivityWatch.
  • Phone location: sent from a location app on your phone directly to your own computer.
  • Calendar: downloaded from a calendar link you provide.
  • Voice memos: recordings that your own iCloud account syncs to your computer, then transcribed on your computer. The audio and text are not sent anywhere else.

Other services Health Sync contacts

Health Sync contacts the services below. None of them receive your Google Health data.

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Overpass and an Overpass mirror run by Kumi Systems) When you stop somewhere that is not one of your saved places The coordinates of that stop, to look up its name (for example, a café). Results are cached so each place is looked up once.
OpenStreetMap map tiles When you point at a place on your day view to see it on a map Requests for map images around that place, which reveal its approximate location.
Google Fonts and unpkg.com When you open a Health Sync page (fonts) or a map (unpkg) Standard web requests for the page's font and map software, which include your IP address. No data from the page is sent.
Your calendar provider If you add a calendar link, a few times a day A request to download that calendar.
Hugging Face If you use voice memos: when the speech-recognition model is downloaded or checked for updates A request for the model files. No audio or text is sent.
Apple iCloud If you use voice memos Your recordings, through your own iCloud account and under Apple's privacy policy.

Each of these services handles the requests it receives under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Stop access: remove Health Sync at myaccount.google.com/permissions. It can then no longer read your Google Health data.
  • Delete your data: delete the Health Sync folder on your computer. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
  • Turn sources off: each optional source can be left out or switched off in the settings.
